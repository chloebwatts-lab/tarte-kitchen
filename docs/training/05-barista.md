# Barista Booklet: Both Venues

*Baristas and coffee window. Read with The Tarte Way. 6 minutes.*

## Your bar

You pour the most-consumed thing Tarte makes. Our house blend is roasted exclusively for us by Parallel Roasters, with a rotating single origin and filter. Matcha is ceremonial grade and we are known for it. You start at 6am at both venues and walk straight into the takeaway hour, coffee and pastry almost exclusively. For hundreds of locals a day, the flat white at our window IS Tarte. Nobody remembers how good the pastry was if the coffee was bitter.

## The standard

1. **Dial in at open, taste, and re-check after rush changes.** Our negative reviews say "bitter", "watery", "no crema". None of those is a machine problem, they are a checking problem.
2. **Milk is textured for the drink**, never screamed, never re-steamed. A cappuccino and a latte from us should be visibly different things.
3. **Every drink leaves the bar looking deliberate.** Wiped cup, level lid, right size marked.
4. **Taste your own shots through the day.** You cannot correct what you have not tasted.
5. **Matcha whisked properly, correct dose.** "Watery matcha" is in our reviews and it is fixable in five seconds of care.
6. **Wait honesty at the window:** if it blows past 10 minutes, the queue hears it from you first.
7. **The size rule:** if a guest questions their cup size or their milk, remake or upsize on the spot, cheerfully, zero debate. We had a 2-star from staff insisting a cup was correct. Winning that argument cost more than a thousand large lattes.

## Sell like Tarte (name one thing)

Coffee has the highest attach potential in the company. You are not often on the till, so these are for whenever you are, or whenever you are chatting to the line at the window. One named suggestion:

- **The pairing:** "muffin top with that? Still warm." The muffin top plus coffee is our most-bought pair for a reason.
- **The extra shot:** guests already order thousands of them, offer it to the tired ones: "make it a strong one?"
- **Cold foam add-ons** on iced drinks: vanilla bean, dulce de leche, matcha. Name one, not the list.
- **The beans:** anyone who compliments the coffee hears "we sell these, 250g or a kilo, ground how you like." A bag of beans puts Tarte on their bench for a month.
- **The regulars' move:** remember their order. "Usual?" is the most powerful word in coffee.

## What our reviews tell us

Coffee is a top-three praise theme at Burleigh (318 positive mentions) and a real complaint theme at both venues when we drift (bitter, watery, lukewarm, no crema, watery matcha). The gap between the two is not talent, it is the dial-in, the taste-check and the remake-without-argument.

Named baristas show up in five-star reviews. Say your name to your regulars.

## Non-negotiables

1. Dialled in and tasted before the first sold cup.
2. Milk right for the drink, no re-steaming.
3. Remake without debate, every time, size, milk or taste.
4. Matcha treated with the same care as espresso.
5. Window waits named out loud past 10 minutes.
6. Bar spotless in motion, not just at close.
7. One named pairing offered, then stop.

## Systems you touch

- Clock in/out on the venue iPad with your PIN.
- Milk and bar restock counts on Tarte Kitchen when rostered to them.
- 86s (a milk, a bean, a syrup): call it to FOH the moment it happens.
