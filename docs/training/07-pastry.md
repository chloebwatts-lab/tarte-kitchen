# Pastry Booklet: Both Venues

*Pastry team. Read with The Tarte Way. 5 minutes.*

## Your section

You start first (around 4am at both venues) and you make the things Tarte is famous for: crullers, viennoiserie, tarts, muffin tops, cookies and the bagels. We bake right through the day, not just for open. A national food writer called us her favourite bakery on the coast. Pastry is also the strongest-margin work in the company, so a tray that goes out short or imperfect costs more here than anywhere else.

## The standard

1. **Consistency beats brilliance.** The Saturday cruller and the Tuesday cruller are twins. Regulars buy the memory of last time.
2. **The counts are the contract.** Bake to the pars and the rotation plan. Selling out by 10 is by design for crullers, selling out of muffin tops at 8am is a par miss, flag it.
3. **No imperfect product reaches the cabinet.** Glaze set, lamination open, no casualties out front. You mess it up, you do not use it: imperfect product goes to the wastage log with a reason, never to the shelf.
4. **The cabinet is your shopfront.** Trays land faced, labelled, allergen cards right. A cabinet that looks perfect sells itself, FOH just has to point.
5. **Keep the display window spotless.** It is the frame to the art.
6. **Taste, taste, taste your product.** Taste what you are making. Taste the prep others have made before you build on it. Taste what you are restocking for service. If you would not put your name on it, do not put ours. You are also the source of FOH's selling lines, tell the opening team what is singing today.
7. **Pest control is everyone's.** Fly fans on, workspace clean, and any sign of a problem goes straight to the FOH manager. Flies near a cabinet appear in our worst reviews.

## What sets us apart

- We are insanely famous for our product. Muffin tops and cookies outsell everything else because they are perfect every time.
- Quality is what sets us apart. Never settling, even for a cookie.
- Quality of ingredients is everything.
- Your crullers are also our service-recovery gift: FOH may give one away to fix a bad moment, so a few late-day crullers matter more than they look.
- Allergen changes in any recipe get flagged to the matrix the same day, before the item sells.

## Systems you touch

- Clock in/out: venue iPad, your PIN.
- Tarte Kitchen: pastry prep list, rotation entries, restock counts, wastage log, cooling logs where they apply.
- Recipe cards print from Tarte Kitchen. Bake to the card, and if the card is wrong, say so, do not silently fix it.
