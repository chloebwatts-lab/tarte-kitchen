# FOH Booklet: Tarte Beach House, Currumbin

*Cafe and Restaurant front of house. Read with The Tarte Way. 8 minutes. Restaurant floor staff also carry the Sequence of Service.*

## Your venue

Waterfront on Currumbin Creek, four spaces, and guests mix them up daily. You never should:

- **The Cafe**, down on the sand. Walk-in, dog friendly, from 6:30am. Casual counter service.
- **The Restaurant**, from 7am. Bookings via the website (Now Book It), walk-ins always welcome. Full table service.
- **The Tea Garden**, beside the Restaurant. High tea Wed to Sun, has its own booklet.
- **The Hideout**, above, through the hundred-year-old fig tree. Functions and private dining, pre-booked only.

Hours: Mon to Fri to 3pm, weekends to 4pm. Cafe happy hour 2 to 3pm ($15). Daytime venue: dinner is "not yet, but watch this space."

## Cafe on the sand

Counter sequence is the Burleigh one: greet in 30 seconds, know the cabinet, allergies early, repeat back, name the wait, farewell. Plus three local rules:

1. **Dogs get water without asking.** Dog-friendly is a review magnet, be the reason.
2. **Families first.** Learn the kids meals by heart and offer them straight away. There is no printed kids menu, you list the options.
3. **Weather turns fast on the creek, and the Cafe has no indoor seating.** If rain rolls in, call a manager straight away, they own what happens next (Restaurant seats if any exist, covers, takeaway boxes), while you keep talking to your tables. An unmanaged rain scramble appears word for word in a 1-star review.

## Restaurant floor

The full sequence lives in the Sequence of Service (its own laminated page). The spine of it:

1. Greet at the door immediately, host or not.
2. Seat, explain table service, **introduce your waiter by name**, share current waits.
3. Water down first. Drinks offered while they browse.
4. Order: allergies early, upsell one side or starter by name, **repeat back**, mark starters vs mains.
5. Mains land together, hot, complete. Check back within 5 minutes.
6. Clear as you go. Nothing sits once they finish.
7. **The last act is several visits, not one.** Offer a second round of coffees, top up wine glasses, tempt a margarita on a sunny afternoon. Dessert menu down, and the closer nobody else on the coast has: "fresh pastries to take home with you?" We are a bakery, the visit can leave with them.
8. Bill to the table, farewell at the door.

Why the name matters: Bailey, Pearl, Hannah and more are named in our five-star reviews again and again. Guests do not review a shirt colour, they review a name.

## Sell like Tarte (name one thing)

- **Table sits down:** "the sourdough and hot honey butter is the way to start here." Our best-selling starter, and it buys the kitchen time on a busy pass.
- **Weekend brunch:** "Grande Mimosa while you decide?" It outsells every other drink on the floor.
- **Bigger appetites:** steak frites, lobster roll, crab linguine. Sell them with their story (next section), the story is what qualifies the price.
- **Any brunch dish:** one side, named: "bacon or the potato hash with that?"
- **After mains:** the cookie skillet or a custard shake for the kids. Dessert is offered, never assumed and never skipped.
- **Kids:** name the kids meals straight away and get them out fast.

One suggestion per moment. Stop at one.

## What our reviews tell us (your venue specifically)

4.55 across 1,688 reviews, the best-rated thing Tarte does, and service is still our #1 complaint. The exact patterns, from real reviews:

- **Waits without words.** 30 to 60 minute waits appear constantly, and the anger is never about the minutes, it is about silence. Name it, update it, own it.
- **Staggered meals.** Two guests finished before the others were served. Flag split timing to the pass before it happens, or warn the table first.
- **Dismissed complaints.** Wrong milk, wrong size, and staff argued. If a guest questions it, remake it. We do not litigate a coffee.
- **Dietary requests ignored at events and high tea.** Booked dietaries are confirmed at seating, out loud.
- **Reserved tables given away with no recovery.** That is a manager-now situation, plus HEARD from you.

## Non-negotiables

1. Door greet immediately, even without a host.
2. Waiter introduced by name at seating.
3. Allergies and booked dietaries confirmed early, out loud.
4. Order repeated back, every time.
5. Waits told before they ask, updated when they change.
6. Check-back within 5 minutes of mains. Clear as you go.
7. Bill to the table. Farewell at the door, invite them back.
8. We upsell something, every table. One named thing, never "anything else?"
