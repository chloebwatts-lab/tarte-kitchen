# The Tarte Way

*Every staff member, every venue. Read before your first shift. 10 minutes.*

## Who we are

Everything that can be made on site is made on site. Ethical, sustainable, free range. Made by us. Crullers fried and glazed fresh, bagels boiled and baked here, tarts, muffin tops, cookies and scones from our own pastry team, and we bake right through the day, not just at dawn. Coffee roasted for us alone by Parallel Roasters. The two exceptions: croissants and sourdough come from a local artisan baker we trust. Guests can feel whether you know all this.

**Our motto: we are craftspeople looking after people.**

Service is getting the order right. Hospitality is how you make the person feel while you do it. Service is a monologue. Hospitality is a dialogue. We are hired for hospitality and trained for service, in that order.

## The Core 4

Four behaviours, every interaction, checkable in three seconds:

1. **Eye contact.**
2. **Smile.**
3. **Energy in your voice.** Guests hear flat.
4. **Stay present.** No drifting mid-order, no chatting over a guest's shoulder.

## The 3 steps of every interaction

1. **Warm greeting within 30 seconds.** If you are slammed, eye contact and "won't be a moment" counts.
2. **Anticipate.** Radar on: a guest hovering over the cabinet reading labels has an allergy question. A guest glancing at their watch needs the wait time now. A dog on the lead at the Cafe gets water without asking.
3. **Fond farewell.** Thank them, invite them back. The farewell is the most skipped step in every cafe on earth, which is exactly why it is remembered.

Regulars get their names used and their usuals remembered. Collect those details and share them with the team.

## How we speak

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| "It's a pleasure" | "No worries" |
| "I'll find out for you" | "I'm not sure" |
| "Fresh batch, we bake right through the day" | "They were baked this morning" (we bake all day, never say just the morning) |
| "Kitchen's about 25 minutes at the moment, worth the wait" | Letting them find out |

And one selling rule that covers everything: **never ask "anything else?" Name one thing, pair it with what they ordered, mean it, then stop.** One suggestion is hospitality. Two is pressure.

## When something goes wrong: HEARD

A mistake handled well is remembered better than no mistake at all. You are writing the final chapter of their visit.

- **H: Hear.** Let them finish. Do not interrupt, do not explain yet.
- **E: Empathise.** "That's not the visit we wanted you to have."
- **A: Apologise.** Sincerely, first, regardless of whose fault. The most common phrase in our worst reviews is "no apology". Never argue, never correct a guest's version of events, never "well actually".
- **R: Resolve.** Fix it now. You may remake or replace anything on the spot without asking a manager. Money off the bill is a manager call, so get one fast.
- **D: Delight.** A small unexpected touch on the way out if you can. The go-to gift is a cruller: it is the signature, guests love it, and it is the friendliest thing we can give away.

Allergy issues, food safety, or anything you cannot fix in two minutes: manager, immediately.

## Facts you must have straight

- **Crullers:** vanilla bean and custard, cinnamon, dulce de leche. Fried and glazed fresh at Burleigh, and when they are gone they are gone.
- **Croissants and sourdough** come from a local artisan baker. Everything else, the crullers, tarts, muffin tops, cookies, scones and our bagels, is made in house, and we bake right through the day.
- **Never claim a house sourdough starter.** Loaves are deliberately priced barely above cost.
- **Coffee** is our exclusive house blend from Parallel Roasters. Matcha is ceremonial grade.
- **Dinner?** "Not yet, but watch this space." Beach House is a daytime place, doors close mid-afternoon.
- **Beach House has four spaces:** the Cafe on the sand (walk-in, dog friendly, from 6:30am), the Restaurant (from 7am, bookings via the website, walk-ins welcome), the Tea Garden (high tea, Wed to Sun), and The Hideout above, looking through a hundred-year-old fig tree (functions and private dining).
- **Burleigh is walk-in only, always.** The queue out the door (weekends, and plenty of weekdays) is by design. Our job is to make it feel looked after.
- **On price**, be confident, never apologetic: everything is made here daily by qualified people from top-shelf ingredients. We are cheaper than plenty of places that buy in frozen.

## Our promise back to you

You will be trained properly by a named person, never left to "just watch". You will taste what you sell. Your roster and your hours live in Tarte Shifts where you can see them. And when a guest names you in a review, the whole team hears about it at line-up, because guests do this weekly and we read them.

- Clock in and out on the venue iPad with your 4-digit PIN. It takes a photo, that is normal.
- See your shifts anytime: log in with your last name + PIN.
- Daily line-up is 5 minutes before open and it is part of your shift.
- Food safety: temp checks, cooling logs and cleaning checklists live on the kitchen iPad (Tarte Kitchen). If you are asked to complete one, it is a legal record, do it honestly and on time.
