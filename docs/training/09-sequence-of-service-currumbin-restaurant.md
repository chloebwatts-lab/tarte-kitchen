# Front of House: Sequence of Service

*Tarte Beach House Restaurant, Currumbin. Print and laminate. Based on the original by the Currumbin FOH manager.*

## HOST: Arrival

- Greet guests at the door immediately. No host free? Acknowledge them and let them know someone will be right over. Nobody stands at our door unseen.
- Escort guests to their table.
- Explain table service, introduce their waiter by name, and share current food, coffee and market wait times, plus the pastries and starters on offer today.
- Tell the section waiter a new table has been sat.

## WAITER: Welcome & Drinks

- Water down straight away, or ask another team member if you are mid-table.
- Introduce yourself by name (guests review names, not shirts), mention wait times, specials and any 86'd items.
- Offer coffee or drinks to start while they browse the menu.
- Once drinks are ordered, check back for starters. The sourdough and hot honey butter is the natural first suggestion. If a starter is ordered, bring pastry plates straight away.
- Mark every dish as starter or main on the docket so the pass can run the table cleanly.

## WAITER: Taking the Order

- **Ask about allergies and dietary needs early**, never as an afterthought. Booked dietaries get confirmed out loud.
- Take the food order. Upsell one side by name: "would you like bacon or the potato hash with that?" beats "do you want to add any sides?"
- **Repeat the order back.** Catch mistakes before the kitchen does.
- Advise of any updated wait times.
- Clear menus from the table and return them to the host station neatly.

## WAITER: After Food Arrives

- If the table had starters, clear all plates and cutlery and reset clean settings for mains.
- Mains land together. If the kitchen flags a stagger, the table hears it from you before the first plate lands.
- **Within 5 minutes** of everyone receiving their meals, check back that everything is correct and complete, then tell the pass the check-back docket can be spiked.
- Flag any problem to a manager fast, and say exactly what is missing or needed. Apologise first, fix second, argue never.
- Offer a drink top-up before the current one is finished.
- Clear glasses and coffee cups as you go.

## WAITER: As the Table Finishes

- Clear plates and cutlery as you go. Nothing sits on the table once guests are done.
- Offer takeaway boxes if food was not finished.
- **The last act is several visits, not one.** Offer a second round of coffees, top up wine glasses, tempt a margarita on a sunny afternoon.
- Offer the dessert menu and today's pastries, and the closer nobody else has: "fresh pastries to take home with you?" We are a bakery, we are never just dessert.
- If dessert is ordered, bring dessert spoons and napkins.
- When they are all set, let them know you will bring the bill to the table whenever they are ready. This matters: it stops the crowd forming at the front door.

## WAITER: Payment & Farewell

- Print the bill and bring it to the table to check.
- Return with the EFTPOS machine and ask if they would like to split or pay together.
- Ask how everything was. If they are glowing, invite a Google review and give them your name.
- Offer a receipt.
- Say thank you and goodbye on the way out, and invite them back.

**Every step, every table, every time. That is how we deliver 5-star service.**

# Our Non-Negotiables: Front of House

**The 8 things we never skip:**

1. Ask about allergies and dietary needs, early, every single table.
2. Repeat the order back before it goes to the kitchen.
3. Check back within 5 minutes of food arriving.
4. Clear plates promptly. Nothing lingers once they are done.
5. Keep guests updated on wait times, always.
6. We upsell something, every table. One named thing, never "anything else?"
7. Bills at tables.
8. Say thank you and goodbye, and welcome guests back another time, every table.

**Know the protein stories, word perfect.** The story is what qualifies the price: Wagyu striploin marble score 3 plus, grain fed. Queensland spanner crab, hand picked. Coral Coast barramundi, sustainably sourced. Ethically sourced Norwegian smoked salmon. Free range chicken. New Zealand gurnard, firm, mild and slightly sweet, in light tempura. The lobster roll needs no species story, sell the indulgence.

**Be friendly and bubbly.** Personality goes a long way. A warm welcome and genuine energy make guests feel looked after before the food even arrives.

**Ask for help.** If your section is overwhelming you and service is starting to slip, asking for help is a strength. Reach out to Carmen, Georgia or Chloe. Remember, your section is your priority before you are available to help others.

**Communicate, communicate, communicate.** Guests are far more forgiving of a wait when they are kept in the loop. Talk to your tables, talk to the team, and communicate clearly with each other.

**Own your section and ask questions.** Teamwork matters and helping each other is encouraged, but stay on top of your own tables first. Your section is your responsibility. And if you are ever unsure about anything, a dish, a process, a guest request, ask. There are no silly questions. Asking is how we all get better together.
