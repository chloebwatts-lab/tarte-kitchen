# FOH Booklet: Tarte Bakery & Cafe, Burleigh

*Counter, floor, takeaway window and juice bar. Read with The Tarte Way. 8 minutes.*

## Your shop

The original. 1748 Gold Coast Highway. Walk-in only, seven days, 6:30am to 3pm, takeaway window from 6am, kitchen closes 2pm, alcohol from 10am. Busiest bakery counter on this stretch of coast, with a queue out the door on weekends and plenty of weekdays. The queue is by design. A queue that is talked to feels like an event. A queue that is ignored writes reviews about it.

## Your shift rhythm

Setup from 5:30. Takeaway window opens 6am, baristas on from 6, the whole venue at 6:30. First hour is takeaway coffee and pastry. Brunch builds from 7:30. The cabinet is the star of the room: it gets faced, wiped and restocked all day, never picked-over looking.

## The counter sequence

1. **Greet within 30 seconds**, even mid-task. Queue building? Someone walks the line with menus and a hello. Wait times are shared at the time of order, never called down the queue.
2. **Know the cabinet.** You have tasted everything in it. When someone hesitates, one confident sentence sells it: "the dulce cruller is the one I'd fight you for."
3. **Take the order, ask allergies early** on any food order, not as an afterthought.
4. **Repeat the order back.** Every mistake caught here is a refund saved and a review saved.
5. **Name the wait honestly** and where to wait or sit.
6. **Farewell.** "Enjoy, see you next weekend." Ten words, five stars.

Floor service: water down first, clear as you go, wipe tables the moment they empty. Flies and dirty tables appear in our worst reviews, so the cabinet area and outdoor tables get walked every 15 minutes.

## Sell like Tarte (name one thing)

These are our proven movers. Pick the one that fits, say it with a reason, stop at one.

- **Coffee ordered?** Pair a pastry: "muffin top with that? Still warm." The muffin top is our single best seller.
- **Pastry box for the road:** almond croissant, choc chip cookie, strawberry tarte, crullers. "The crullers never last the day, grab one now."
- **Any brunch order:** one side. "Would you like bacon or halloumi with that?" beats "any sides?" every time.
- **Leaving with nothing in hand:** a loaf. "Fresh sourdough to take home?"
- **Weekend late morning:** a mimosa or Bloody Mary with brunch, from 10am.
- **Juice bar:** the MYO juice is the hero, offer ginger as the add.

Never push a second item. The upsell is information from a person who has tasted it, not a script.

## What our reviews tell us (your shop specifically)

What earns 5 stars here: the pastries, the coffee, and staff by name. "Ask for Barney to help with choices" is a real review. Being known by name in a review is the highest compliment in this company.

What loses stars here, in order: **feeling overpriced** (fix: warmth and confidence, a $7 pastry with a story beats a $7 pastry in silence), **waits with no communication**, and **stale or cold items making it to a plate** (if you would not pay for it, do not serve it, tell the kitchen).

The biggest one: **guests who complained and felt brushed off.** When someone flags a problem, run HEARD. Hear them out without interrupting. Empathise. Apologise, sincerely, before anything else. Then fix it on the spot. The apology matters more than the fix, our worst reviews are about being dismissed, not about the food.

One more from a real 1-star: a guest asked us to warm a croissant and got a flat no. In a rush we genuinely cannot warm pastries, but the answer is never a bare no. Explain kindly, offer what we can do. Small, reasonable asks get a yes wherever possible.

## Non-negotiables

1. Nobody waits 30 seconds unacknowledged, including the queue.
2. Allergies asked early on every food order.
3. Order repeated back, every time.
4. Waits named before they ask, updated if they blow out.
5. Cabinet faced and full-looking, tables wiped inside 2 minutes.
6. Complaints get an apology first, a fix second, an argument never.
7. Every guest farewelled.
8. We upsell something, every order. One named thing, never "anything else?"

## Systems you touch

- Clock in/out: venue iPad, your PIN.
- Cleaning checklists and closing duties: kitchen iPad, Tarte Kitchen, tick as you go, they are legal records.
- 86'd items: the moment you hear it, the whole counter knows it.
