# Tea Garden Booklet: High Tea, Currumbin

*Tea Garden FOH. Read with The Tarte Way. 6 minutes.*

## Your room

Beside the Restaurant, Wed to Sun from 9am, closed Mon and Tue. High tea is $55pp with a drink included, 90-minute sittings, booked ahead via the website. Walk-ins can take the pastry bar and a la carte. Private high teas run here (+$500, up to 24 guests) and in The Hideout ($89pp, 12 to 32 guests). This is the most occasion-driven room Tarte has: birthdays, baby showers, mother-daughter dates. People book us weeks out and dress up. The bar is higher here and the reviews show it: 4.83 average, and we protect that.

## The high tea sequence

1. **Confirm the occasion at booking check.** Birthday? Say the word "happy birthday" at the table. It costs nothing and makes the review.
2. **Dietaries were booked, so confirm them out loud at seating.** A pre-booked dietary that gets missed is our single worst high tea failure, it is in real reviews. Check the docket, check the kitchen, check the table.
3. **Seat, explain the flow:** 90 minutes, drink included, how the tiers arrive, cloud drink upgrade for $2.50.
4. **Drinks land within 10 minutes.** At a fixed price sitting, a late drink feels like theft. An event room that is understaffed and silent is our most expensive kind of 1-star.
5. **Tiers explained when presented**, bottom up, savoury to sweet, allergen swaps pointed out on the tier.
6. **Top-ups walked, not waited for.** Tea pots refreshed, water refreshed, never let a table run dry mid-sitting.
7. **The 75-minute mark:** dessert-tier check-in and the gentle runway: "no rush at all, whenever you're ready I'll bring the bill over."
8. **Farewell at the gate.** Occasion guests get a proper send-off, and mention the private hire if they loved it: "we do these privately for groups too."

## Sell like Tarte

- **Cloud drink upgrade** at seating: "for $2.50 the Cookie Tiramisu cloud is the move." Half the room says yes when it is named.
- **Bubbles:** mimosa or a glass of Fierce prosecco on arrival, Veuve for the big occasions.
- **Extra scone round** mid-sitting when a table is flying through the tiers.
- **The retail shelf:** jam jars, cups and saucers, candles. "The jam on your scone is that jar on the shelf." Homewares only sell when someone says that sentence.
- **Gift vouchers** on the way out for the guest who says "mum would love this".
- **Private hire** whenever a table mentions a group, a shower, a birthday crowd.

## Non-negotiables

1. Booked dietaries confirmed out loud at seating, every table.
2. First drinks within 10 minutes of sitting.
3. Tiers explained, allergen swaps pointed out.
4. No table runs dry. Top-ups are walked.
5. Occasions acknowledged by name.
6. The 90 minutes is managed with grace, never a shot clock.
7. Farewell at the gate, voucher and private hire offered when it fits.

## Facts for this room

- Speciality tea pots $6, scones with raspberry jam and cream are the pastry bar hero.
- Kids high tea exists, know it before the family sits down.
- Closed Mon and Tue: offer the Restaurant next door without missing a beat.
