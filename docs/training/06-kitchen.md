# Kitchen Booklet: Chefs + KP, Both Venues

*Every kitchen and KP role. Read with The Tarte Way. 7 minutes.*

## Your kitchen

Almost everything on the pass is made in-house from scratch, and the menu says so. Burleigh's kitchen serves 6:30am to 2pm. At Beach House the Cafe kitchen serves 6:30 to 2 and the Restaurant kitchen 7 to 2, both rolling to the afternoon menu from 2 to 3. Volume is real: brunch dishes here sell in the thousands per season. The kitchen is where our reviews are won before FOH ever gets a chance.

## The pass standard

Guests forgive a wait they were warned about. They never forgive these four, and all four are in our real reviews:

1. **Hot food leaves hot, cold food leaves cold.** Cold eggs and lukewarm plates are review killers. If it sat, it gets refired, not sent.
2. **A table's mains land together.** Two guests finishing before the others are served is one of our ugliest recurring reviews. If a table must stagger, the pass tells FOH before the food moves, so the table hears it from us first.
3. **Nothing undercooked, nothing stale, nothing sloppy leaves.** Undercooked eggs, stale bread and messy presentation are all in our 1-stars. The plate you would not pay $26 for does not go.
4. **Remakes are instant and blameless.** FOH may request a remake without a debate. The argument never wins, the remake always does.

**Check-back docket:** FOH confirms the table is complete and correct within 5 minutes, then the docket is spiked. If something is missing, the pass hears exactly what, and it jumps the queue.

**Taste, taste, taste.** Taste what you are making. Taste the prep others have made before you build on it. Taste what you are restocking for service. If you would not put your name on it, do not put ours.

## Allergies are law

- Allergy dockets are called out loud and acknowledged out loud.
- The allergen matrix lives in Tarte Kitchen and printed by the pass. If you are not sure, you stop and check, you never guess.
- Ignored dietaries at events and high tea have cost us publicly. Booked dietaries are prepped before the sitting, not solved during it.
- Separate boards, fresh gloves, clean fryer calls where they apply. An allergy mistake is the one error we cannot apologise our way out of.

## Timing and comms

- **86 early, not when it is gone.** The moment a line item is at risk, FOH knows.
- **Wait times live at the pass.** The Head of Pass chef and the FOH pass leader own the number together off the bump screen, and FOH quotes only what the pass has confirmed. The moment the screen slips past what is being quoted, the two of you reset the number.
- **Prep off the prep sheet**, it is forecast from real sales history. Par is par.

**The two heads of pass are a team, full stop.** The Head of Pass chef and the FOH pass leader must gel, and the whole service depends on it. No friction across the pass during service, ever: if you disagree, run the service, then work it out afterwards. And remember the FOH pass leader is trained for exactly this and has the best view of the room, do not argue with them.

## Food safety (legal records, non-negotiable)

- Temp checks, cooling logs and cleaning checklists run on the kitchen iPad (Tarte Kitchen), on time, honestly. Cold chain under 5°C, hot holding over 60°C, cooling logged through the curve.
- These records are what council sees. A blank cell is a failed inspection waiting.
- Your venue's Food Safety Supervisors are named on the noticeboard. New symptoms, cuts, illness: tell the FSS before you touch food.
- **Wastage is logged, always**, with the real reason. Overproduction data is how we fix pars instead of blaming people.

## KP

The dish pit sets the kitchen's pace and the room's plates. Clean-as-you-go beats hero sessions. Glasses polished, cutlery checked, nothing chipped reaches a table.

A clean kitchen is the job, not the chore after the job. The clean after service matters as much as the service itself: take pride in it, do it properly the first time, and if you need help, ask. A kitchen that closes spotless opens fast tomorrow.

## Systems you touch

- Clock in/out: venue iPad, your PIN.
- Tarte Kitchen iPad: checklists, cooling logs, prep sheet, restock counts, wastage log.
- Purchase orders and stock: managers draft them, your counts feed them, count honestly.
