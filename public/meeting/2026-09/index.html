Part two

The app. Every tile, live, on this screen.

kitchen.tarte.com.au on every iPad and phone. One line on what each page is for, then the real thing, then why it matters. Fifteen minutes.

The iPad home screen

Everything staff need is five tiles. Bookmark it, it stays logged in for 90 days.

Open in a tab kitchen.tarte.com.au/staffaccess
Why it mattersOne front door on every iPad and phone. Checklists, stock, breakages, and the two password areas for managers and Oliver. If someone asks where something is, the answer is always this screen.
Checklists and logs

Cleaning and food temperature checklists, by venue, by area, with what is due today.

Open in a tab kitchen.tarte.com.au/kitchen
Why it mattersThis is our food safety evidence. Council can ask for it any day. Weekly and monthly lists stay open all cycle and email Chloe what was missed at the end of it.
Checklists and logs

The cooling log times every cooked batch going into the coolroom.

Open in a tab kitchen.tarte.com.au/kitchen/cooling
Why it mattersThe HACCP rule is 21 degrees within two hours and 5 degrees within six. A missed entry looks the same as unsafe food to an inspector. Thirty seconds per batch.
Checklists and logs

Log anything binned, as it happens. Item, how much, why.

Open in a tab kitchen.tarte.com.au/log
Why wastage is integralWastage is the only cost we can see the moment it happens. Every entry is costed on the spot from our own recipe prices. Logged waste sets the ordering pars, shows which prep is over-made, and is the first number Oliver and the head chefs check on Friday. Under 1.5% of revenue is green, over 2.5% is red. Unlogged waste still costs the same, we just cannot fix it.
Checklists and logs

Pastry rotation: prepared, sold and discarded for every bake.

Open in a tab kitchen.tarte.com.au/kitchen/pastry
Why it mattersBurleigh sells about 11,000 pastries a month. One tray over-baked a day is $10,000 a year. This log tells us which bake to cut and which to add.

Stock and ordering

Restock and prep counts. The closing chef counts, the prep chef makes exactly that.

Open in a tab kitchen.tarte.com.au/kitchen/restock
Why chefs must use thisThe count is the prep list. Each line has a number, a priority order and, new from today, a needed-by day and time. If it is on the sheet it gets made first thing. If it is not, it is a 6am phone call and a dish we cannot serve at lunch. Two minutes at close saves an hour in the morning.

People

Every new hire goes through the onboarding link. No paper, no Deputy invite.

How it worksManager sends the hire link. The new starter fills a seven step form on their phone. Manager approves. Deputy, Xero and the staff list are created for them, and the system chases anything missing.
WhyOne missed tax form or bank detail costs Shawna an hour and the new starter a pay week. Live since 16 September.

  1. Leave requests for December and January in by Friday 16 October.Rosters for the holidays are published by mid November.

Training

Every department has a handbook. Take yours home today.

What is in the packThe Tarte Way for everyone, then one booklet per department: front of house Burleigh, front of house Beach House, Tea Garden, barista, kitchen, pastry and managers. The Currumbin sequence of service is in there too.
The last page of each one is a Reset CardOne page. Read it when service has gone flat or a review says we were slow. It is the refresher, not the training.
What we askRead yours this week. New starters get theirs on day one from their manager and it is ticked off in Staff training. Managers, the system manual is yours alone.
